# T028 Product Contract And Research

- Status: Completed
- Date: 2026-08-02

The owner-approved product refoundation is recorded as the canonical v0.2
development contract. Modary targets lightweight componentized modular
monoliths for Go business backends and administrative systems. Core is
database-free; PostgreSQL, Identity, RBAC, River, Audit, Governed Actions, MCP,
and Admin are optional components or Profiles.

Primary Gin-Vue-Admin repository evidence and relevant Issue themes support the
weight, generated-code ownership, initialization, and integration decisions.
The research explicitly distinguishes historical reports from verified current
repository measurements.

The final contract also separates ordinary SQL repository transactions from
sealed Governed Action authority and clarifies that component removal never
silently destroys previously applied database objects or consumer data.
